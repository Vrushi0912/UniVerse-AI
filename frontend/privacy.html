UniVerse AI

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to UniVerse AI. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our AI assistant platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Key Point: UniVerse AI operates entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any servers.

2. Information We DON'T Collect

UniVerse AI is designed with privacy in mind. We do NOT:

  • ❌ Collect your personal information on remote servers
  • ❌ Track your browsing activity
  • ❌ Use cookies for tracking or analytics
  • ❌ Share your data with third parties
  • ❌ Store your conversations on our servers
  • ❌ Monitor or analyze your usage patterns
  • ❌ Sell or rent your information

3. Local Data Storage

All your data is stored locally in your web browser using localStorage technology:

What is Stored Locally:

  • Account Information: Name, email, and encrypted password
  • Chat History: Your conversations with the AI
  • Settings: API key, theme preference, selected AI model
  • Session Data: Login status and user ID

Important:

This data remains on YOUR device and is never uploaded to any server. Clearing your browser data will permanently delete this information.

4. API Usage

UniVerse AI uses OpenRouter API to access AI models:

How It Works:

  • You provide your own OpenRouter API key
  • Your messages are sent directly to OpenRouter's servers
  • We do not intercept or store these communications
  • OpenRouter's privacy policy applies to API interactions

API Data Handling:

When you use UniVerse AI, your prompts and AI responses are transmitted through OpenRouter's API. Please review OpenRouter's Privacy Policy for information about how they handle data.

5. File Uploads

When you upload files or photos:

  • Files are processed locally in your browser
  • File contents may be included in API requests (subject to OpenRouter's policy)
  • Files are not stored permanently on any server
  • You can remove attachments before sending

6. Voice Recognition

When you use voice input:

  • Uses your browser's Web Speech API
  • Audio is processed by your browser or operating system
  • We do not record or store audio
  • Browser privacy policies apply to speech recognition

7. Third-Party Services

UniVerse AI integrates with the following third-party services:

OpenRouter API:

  • Purpose: AI model access
  • Data Shared: Your prompts and AI responses
  • Privacy Policy: openrouter.ai/privacy

CDN Services:

  • Highlight.js (code syntax highlighting)
  • Marked.js (markdown rendering)
  • These load libraries from CDN servers

8. Data Security

Local Storage Security:

  • Data stored in browser localStorage
  • Passwords encoded with base64 (⚠️ basic encoding only)
  • Session expires after 7 days
  • Logout clears session data

Security Recommendations:

  • Use a strong, unique password
  • Don't share your device with untrusted users
  • Clear browser data if using a shared computer
  • Keep your API key confidential

Security Limitations:

⚠️ Important: UniVerse AI is an educational project. The password encoding is basic and not suitable for storing sensitive information. For production use, proper encryption and secure backend storage would be required.

9. Children's Privacy

UniVerse AI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are under 13, please do not use this service without parental supervision.

10. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is accessible in browser developer tools
  • Export: Use the export feature to download your conversations
  • Delete: Clear browser data to remove all information
  • Modify: Update your profile and settings at any time

11. Data Deletion

To Delete Your Data:

  1. Log out from UniVerse AI
  2. Clear your browser's localStorage:
    • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cookies and site data
    • Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
    • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All
  3. Or clear data for this site only using browser developer tools

12. No Analytics or Tracking

UniVerse AI does not use:

  • Google Analytics or similar tools
  • Tracking pixels or beacons
  • Advertising cookies
  • Session replay tools
  • Fingerprinting techniques

13. Browser Permissions

UniVerse AI may request browser permissions for:

  • Microphone: For voice input feature (optional)
  • File Access: For file uploads (optional)

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your browser settings.

14. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Educational Purpose

UniVerse AI is created as an educational project to demonstrate:

  • AI integration techniques
  • Modern web development practices
  • Privacy-focused design
  • Client-side data storage

It is not intended for storing sensitive or critical information.
